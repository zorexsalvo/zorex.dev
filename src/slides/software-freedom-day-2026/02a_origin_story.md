# The Origin Story

- Created by **Barry Warsaw** in July 2000
- Explicitly modeled on the **IETF RFC process**
- Barry invented the word "PEP" because he liked funny-sounding words
- Then backronymed it to "Python Enhancement Proposal"
- He even wrote **PEP 0 by hand**
