# Why I Read PEPs

## A story

**PEP 772** — Packaging Council governance process

- Replaces **PEP 609**
- Approved by the Steering Council: **April 16, 2026**
- Creates an elected, accountable body with delegated authority over Python packaging standards

I saw an **election announcement.** I followed it to the PEP.

The PEP told me what was actually happening.

Most Python developers I talk to have never heard of it.

It's public. It's free. It's right there.
