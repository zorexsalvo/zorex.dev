# The PEP Reader's Framework

## The 5-Minute Checklist

1. **Problem:** Is it a real pain point or theoretical?
2. **Motivation:** Is the reasoning sound? Any holes?
3. **Backwards Compatibility:** Does it break anything? How much pain?
4. **Rejection Risk:** Are obvious objections covered in "Rejected Ideas"?

## Your Role in Free Software

Reading PEPs is the first step toward **participation**.
