# Myth #1

## "Only core developers can write PEPs"

### False.

- **PEP 572** was co-authored by Chris Angelico (not a core dev at the time)
- **PEP 3150** was authored by Nick Coghlan (core dev, but still got rejected)
- The barrier is quality of argument, not job title

> "A PEP is a design document, not a badge of authority."

This is **software freedom** in practice.
